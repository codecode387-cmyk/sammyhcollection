The Inception

Bridging Architectural Purity & Kinetic Performance

In 2021, designer Sammy H. observed a growing rift in modern footwear. On one side stood luxury fashion houses creating sculptural silhouettes devoid of all-day ergonomic resilience. On the other stood performance athletic brands using synthetic polymers destined to persist in landfills for centuries.

SammyH was conceived as a synthesis. We set out to prove that orthopedic biomechanics, sustainable raw materials, and runway-level silhouettes could coalesce without compromise. Each collection emerges from prolonged prototyping sessions inside our Porto workshop, balancing millimeters of volume against the human walking gait.

28 Iterations To finalize our core ergonomic archetype in 2021.
Zero Synthetics In contact with the foot interior lining across all drops.
45,000+ Pairs Walked across 62 countries with sub-0.3% return rate.
Our Sanctuary of Making

The Porto Atelier

In Northern Portugal, our third-generation shoemakers merge legacy hand-welted techniques with computerized millimeter tolerances.

The Anatomical Foundation

Traditional sneakers compress the metatarsal bones. Our bespoke lasts mimic the dynamic expansion of the human foot upon strike, widening 3.8mm across the ball of the foot while locking the heel counter firmly in place.

  • check_circle 4-stage digital 3D pressure map analysis
  • check_circle Carved sustainably from aged Nordic hornbeam wood
  • check_circle Calibrated for dynamic arch support without orthotic inserts
Stage 02: 360-Degree Norwegian Hand Stitch
2

Architectural Seam Integrity

Rather than relying solely on toxic petrochemical adhesives that crack after two seasons, each SammyH chassis is anchored using continuous beeswax-coated linen threads that lock the upper directly to the internal footbed.

  • check_circle Resolable up to 5 times over shoe lifecycle
  • check_circle Natural water barrier created by compressed beeswax cord
  • check_circle Fully disassembled for circular end-of-life recycling
Stage 03: Heavy Barrel Chestnut Extract Tanning
3

Zero Chromium Chemistry

Our raw hides originate strictly as agricultural byproducts from certified humane pastoral cooperatives in the Valdarno region. Tanned strictly using bark extracts, mimosa, and quebracho rather than toxic heavy metals.

  • check_circle Certified Leather Working Group (LWG) Gold Standard
  • check_circle Breathable organic porosity that molds to foot warmth
  • check_circle Develops an iridescent, individual patina with wear
Stage 04: Bio-Circulated Ground Contact Chassis
4

Engineered Compound Resilience

Partnering exclusively with Vibram® Italy, our outsoles feature the proprietary SammyH tread architecture: multi-directional drainage lugs paired with 40% post-industrial recycled rubber compound for extraordinary wet-weather traction.

  • check_circle 500-mile guaranteed heel tread longevity
  • check_circle High energy return EVA midsole core
  • check_circle Non-marking natural rubber composition
